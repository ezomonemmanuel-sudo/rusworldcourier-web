How to make a claim

Open a support ticket from your dashboard (or use the contact form) quoting your tracking number, and include photos of the packaging and contents for damage claims.

Time limits

  • Damage or missing contents: report within 7 days of delivery. Keep all packaging.
  • Lost parcel: report once the parcel is 30 days past the end of its estimated transit time without a delivery scan.

What we compensate

  • Insured shipments: up to the declared value you insured, supported by proof of value (invoice or receipt).
  • Uninsured shipments: up to a standard limit of 20 {{ currency }} per kg (maximum 100 {{ currency }} per parcel), or the shipping fee refund where the parcel is lost, whichever is greater.

Exclusions

Prohibited or restricted items; inadequate packing; inherent defects or perishable goods; seizure or delay by authorities; delays alone; indirect or consequential loss; and force majeure events.

Timeline

We acknowledge claims promptly and aim to decide within 30 days of receiving all supporting documents. Approved compensation is paid to the original payer.

Limits above are example values for the site owner to confirm.
