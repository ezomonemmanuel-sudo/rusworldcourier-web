Before booking you must confirm that your parcel contains no prohibited or restricted items. Rules differ by destination; if you're unsure, ask us first.

Prohibited - we will not carry

  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks and weapons of any kind
  • Illegal drugs, narcotics and psychotropic substances; drug paraphernalia
  • Flammable, corrosive, toxic or radioactive materials; compressed gases; lithium batteries shipped loose
  • Cash, bank notes, coins, bearer instruments, gift cards, precious metals and stones
  • Counterfeit or pirated goods and items that infringe intellectual property
  • Human remains, live animals, perishable food and biological samples
  • Obscene or illegal materials, and anything banned by law in the origin, transit or destination country
  • Goods and destinations subject to sanctions or export controls

Restricted - only with approval or documents

  • Electronics containing lithium batteries (installed in the device)
  • Medicines and supplements (prescription and import rules vary)
  • Alcohol, tobacco and vapes
  • Cultural property, antiques, art and items subject to export licences
  • Dual-use or technology items subject to export control

Your responsibility

You are responsible for declaring contents accurately. Inaccurate declarations may result in seizure, fines, return at your cost and account suspension.
