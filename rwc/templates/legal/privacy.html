1. Who we are

{{ company.name }} ("we", "us") operates this website and courier service. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your choices.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, hashed password, SMS-alert preference.
  • Shipment data: sender and recipient names, addresses, phone numbers and optional recipient email; parcel description, weight, dimensions, declared value, delivery notes; tracking events.
  • Payment data: the payment reference, amount, currency, status and channel returned by Paystack. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's pages and are never stored on our servers.
  • Support data: messages you send via the contact form or support tickets.
  • Technical & security data: IP address and actions recorded in security/audit logs (sign-ins, bookings, status changes) to protect accounts and investigate abuse.

3. Why we use it

To quote, book, collect, transport and deliver shipments; to take and confirm payment of shipping fees; to provide tracking and notifications; to provide support; to comply with legal, customs and security obligations; and to prevent fraud and abuse.

4. Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners and carriers who need sender/recipient details to move your parcel, including those in the destination country.
  • Customs and law-enforcement authorities where required by law.
  • Paystack (payment processing) and, if configured, our email/SMS providers.
  • We do not sell personal data.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security cookie used to protect forms from cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Retention

We keep shipment and payment records for as long as needed for the delivery, claims, accounting and legal obligations, then delete or anonymise them. Audit logs are kept to protect the service.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ, the GDPR/UK GDPR or the Nigeria Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your data, to object to processing, and to complain to a regulator. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, CSRF protection, rate limiting, role-based access and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; tell us promptly if you suspect misuse of your account.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
