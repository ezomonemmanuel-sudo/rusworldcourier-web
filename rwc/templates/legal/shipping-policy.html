Collection

Parcels are collected from the pickup address you enter (in Russia) on or after your preferred date, once the shipping fee has been paid and verified. Someone must be present with the parcel, packed and sealed.

Packing and labelling

Use sturdy, new packaging. Print your shipping label from your dashboard and attach it clearly to the largest side. Remove old labels.

Weight and size

  • Maximum 70 kg per parcel, and no side longer than 150 cm.
  • Charges use the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 5,000), rounded up to the next 0.5 kg. If measured weight or size at our hub differs from your booking we may adjust the fee.

Transit times

Estimates are in working days from pickup and are not guaranteed. Customs processing in the destination country may add time.

Customs, duties and taxes

Import duties and taxes are assessed and collected only by the destination customs authority or its official delivery carrier. We never ask recipients for release fees via this site, bank transfer, crypto or gift cards. Provide complete and accurate contents descriptions and values.

Delivery

The delivery partner attempts delivery to the address given. If delivery fails we will contact the sender; unclaimed or undeliverable parcels may be returned at the sender's cost or disposed of where permitted.

Destinations

We ship to the destinations listed in the quote tool. We may refuse or suspend any shipment or destination where required by sanctions, export-control or carrier restrictions.

Address changes

Contact support quickly: address changes may not be possible once a parcel is in transit.
