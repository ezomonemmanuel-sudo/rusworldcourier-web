Our approach

We believe sending a parcel internationally should be clear: a price you can see before you book, a tracking number you can follow, and a support team you can reach.

Our platform gives senders an instant quote calculator, online booking, secure payment of the shipping fee, a printable label and a live tracking timeline. Our dispatchers and riders use the same system to assign pickups and record every status update, with an audit trail behind every change.
